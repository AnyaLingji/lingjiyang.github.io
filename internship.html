01 / INTERNSHIP OVERVIEW

This research internship explored how young women use generative AI within emotional, intimate and imaginative practices. My work focused on role-play, persona construction, desire articulation and the social circulation of intimate scripts across digital platforms.

The internship took place within Intimate AI: Young Women’s Self-Perception and Embodied Knowledge in Times of Automation at Uppsala University. My contribution focused on the ways women construct and exchange AI persona scripts in online communities, treating intimate AI interaction as a socially situated practice shaped by platform cultures and collaborative language.

02 / FEATURED PROJECT / POLYPHONIC DESIRE

Polyphonic Desire: Intimate Role-Playing with AI is a netnographic study of the way women create, exchange and refine AI persona scripts on RedNote. It approaches these scripts as socially and collaboratively formed repertoires of desire, rather than isolated individual fantasies.

The project approached persona scripts as more than instructions for software. Descriptors, prohibitions and desired conversational tones encode assumptions about relationships, agency, gendered expectations and the conditions under which users feel able to experiment with intimate roles. Its title, Polyphonic Desire, refers to the plurality of voices involved in the circulation and reinterpretation of these prompts.

03 / METHODS AND MATERIALS

Contextual Netnography

The research centred on a core corpus of 27 posts shared by 25 creators, with 45 classifiable comments.

Five Linked Datasets

The datasets organised platform metadata, AI persona scripts, lexical descriptors, AI constraint rules and user commentary.

Qualitative and Descriptive Analysis

Coding and textual summaries explored the negotiation of AI-generated responses, user agency and collaborative prompt development.

Five linked datasets were assembled to separate platform metadata, prompt text, lexical descriptors, constraint rules and discussion responses. This organisation supported transparent movement between particular posts and emerging patterns. Qualitative coding and descriptive textual analysis were used to trace user agency, shared conventions and attempts to shape unpredictable AI responses.

04 / THE VOCABULARY OF DESIRE

The original word cloud draws on 94 coded personality descriptors. It brings together qualities including gentleness, steadiness and maturity with more controlling, possessive and dangerous personas. It summarises selected platform materials rather than estimating preferences across all users.

The 94-item descriptor collection contains apparently contrasting preferences: caring or steady characteristics sit alongside possessive, controlling or transgressive personas. The word cloud is a visual summary of the coded language, not a ranking of womens preferences across the wider population.

05 / RESEARCH ETHICS

Public visibility does not eliminate the ethical sensitivities of intimate platform discourse. The research anonymised user records and did not treat private messages, usernames or restricted-group material as automatically publishable.

06 / INTERNSHIP REFLECTION

The internship brought together platform-based qualitative research and questions of intimacy, user agency and digitally mediated desire. It provided methodological and conceptual groundwork for my later master’s thesis.
