Polyphonic Desire

Intimate Role-Playing with AI. Exploring the collective creation, circulation and revision of AI personas on RedNote.

01 / QUESTION02 / FIELDWORK03 / DATA STRUCTURE04 / ANALYSIS & WORD CLOUD05 / ETHICS
01 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Intimacy as an assemblage of desire

Using Deleuze and Guattari’s assemblage theory, the report examines how users, prompt authors, AI models, platform rules and shared language participate in the production of AI-mediated intimacy.

02 / METHOD

Netnographic fieldwork

Observed RedNote AI persona-script practices from 7–24 October 2025. The core material comprises 27 highly engaged posts from 25 creators and 45 classifiable comments. The dataset was curated through documented sampling, cleaning and coding decisions.

04 / INTERPRETATION

Collective production and ongoing revision

The report describes how prompt authors shape reusable persona scripts, users adapt them, and LLMs enact them. Community members collectively adjust instructions and migrate between platforms when model updates or restrictions disrupt their practices.

Its theoretical interpretation connects the horizontal production and expression of desire with territorialization, deterritorialization and reterritorialization over time.

What the persona vocabulary reveals

The frequently used labels combine protective traits such as gentle, mature and steady with power-related traits such as possessive, manipulative and scheming. In the report, this coexistence helps explain how AI personas are jointly imagined around emotional security, control, and the appeal of danger.

05 / RESEARCH ETHICS

Public data, private experiences

The report anonymized user records and excluded materials collected from access-controlled private groups. It discusses the ethical tension between publicly visible content and intimate personal disclosures.

No raw screenshots, usernames, private chat transcripts, or underlying datasets are made public through this portfolio.
