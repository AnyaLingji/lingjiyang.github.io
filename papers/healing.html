COURSEWORK / Scoping review & conceptual framework

Between Healing and Harm

A Dual-Pathway–Three-Dimension Model of Digital Companionship and Mental Health
Health Psychology and Behavioural MedicineVG · Pass with Distinction2025
01 / OVERVIEW

What this work explores

Drawing on 21 selected studies, this paper proposes a conceptual model of how AI companionship may provide emotional support or, under other patterns of use, undermine psychological flexibility.

02 / RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Questions

  1. How does digital companionship produce potentially beneficial and detrimental psychological outcomes?
  2. Which dimensions of psychological flexibility help explain healthier or riskier human–AI companionship?
03 / APPROACH

Research design & material

The paper reports searches across Web of Science, ScienceDirect, Scopus and Wiley Online for research published from 2022 to 2025. From 1,172 initial records, 65 papers progressed after initial screening and 21 studies were retained for matrix-based thematic synthesis. This is a literature synthesis, not an experiment with chatbot users.

04 / THEORETICAL LENS

Conceptual foundations

The three dimensions of psychological flexibility organize the framework: Openness (acceptance and cognitive defusion), Awareness (present-moment awareness and self-as-context), and Engagement (values and committed action). The review links these dimensions to competing patterns of emotional compensation and depletion.

06 / ANALYSIS

Key arguments & findings

Compensatory pathway

Emotional desensitisation → cognitive reappraisal → behavioural transformation. Low-pressure disclosure can support openness; responsive interaction may assist awareness; users may then act in line with their values.

Depletion pathway

Emotional addiction → cognitive narrowing → behavioural atrophy. Excessive reliance on algorithmic reassurance may undermine openness, narrow reflective awareness and reduce social initiative.

Conditions matter

The model argues against describing AI companionship as inherently therapeutic or inherently harmful. Outcomes depend on patterns, contexts and degrees of reliance.

07 / ORIGINAL WORK

Contribution

The paper contributes the Dual-Pathway–Three-Dimension Model, a conceptual synthesis intended to guide research and design on emotional support, dependence and psychological flexibility. The proposed pathways should not be interpreted as experimentally established causal effects.

This is a student research paper, not a peer-reviewed journal publication. Concept diagrams summarize the manuscript and do not constitute additional empirical evidence.
