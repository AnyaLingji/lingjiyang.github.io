## Notes
- Academic works are correctly labeled coursework, thesis or internship, not peer-reviewed publications.
- The 14-day randomized experiment is a pre-analysis plan with simulations, not a completed trial.
- The Dual-Pathway–Three-Dimension Model is conceptual, not experimental causal evidence.
- No identifiable raw research participant materials are included.
- Review your public CV and email before publishing.
- `index.html` → `coursework.html` → a paper page is the recommended browser test.
- To publish, upload the extracted *contents* of this folder to the root of a GitHub Pages repository.
