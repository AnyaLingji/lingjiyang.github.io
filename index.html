01 / MEET LINGJI

Meet Lingji

Hi, I'm Lingji Yang, a media and communication researcher who recently completed a master's degree at Uppsala University in Sweden.

My research explores how emerging technologies shape human experience, particularly how people understand themselves, develop intimate relationships, and negotiate their boundaries with artificial intelligence.

I work across media and communication studies, psychology, and human–computer interaction. My research experience includes netnography, in-depth interviews, and existential phenomenological analysis, alongside academic training in experimental design, causal inference, and statistical analysis using R.

Beyond research, I love video games, dancing, photography, making little things by hand, and discovering new places.
