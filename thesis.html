Digital Transgression

Chinese Women's Kink Interactions with Generative AI

01 / OVERVIEW

What happens when desire meets a digital boundary?

This thesis examines Chinese women's kink-related interactions with generative AI through the concept of digital transgression. It asks how women encounter social, relational, and technological limits, how AI mediates their exploration of these limits, and what these encounters make visible about desire and the self.

The thesis develops an existential media interpretation of intimate AI use, informed by two rounds of interviews with nine women and preparatory online fieldwork.

03 / RESEARCH DESIGN

From public scripts to lived experience.

The study uses three distinct but connected phases. The interview material, not the social-media corpus, constitutes the primary dataset for existential phenomenological analysis.

Phase 01 · Contextual netnographyRedNote persona prompts and role scripts collected in October 2025. From 89 screened posts, 27 posts by 25 creators formed the core corpus. Used for contextual orientation and recruitment, not for prevalence estimates.
01
Phase 02 · Two-round interviewsNine Chinese women participated in two Mandarin-language interviews each, totaling 18 interviews. The first interview was participant-led; the second clarified and developed prior accounts.
02
Phase 03 · Existential phenomenological researchFollowing Churchill (2022): meaning-unit analysis, individual structural descriptions (ISDs), and a general structural description (GSD). The theory was introduced during later interpretation, not as predetermined codes.

04 / THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK

Understanding transgression through existence and mediation.

The thesis develops digital transgression by bringing Georges Bataille's theory of erotic transgression into dialogue with Amanda Lagerkvist's existential media theory, situated within the pressures of late-modern self-realisation.

05 / KEY FINDINGS

Digital transgression is a movement, not a single crossing.

The general structural description identifies intertwined movements through which participants approached, tested, crossed, and sometimes reaffirmed boundaries in AI-mediated intimate experience.

01 · Desire becomes articulablePrompting and revising AI responses helped participants give language to difficult-to-disclose desires, while retaining distance from human social exposure.
01
02 · Fantasy and judgment coexistSome participants found scenarios compelling while continuing to reject their implications for everyday relationships. Erotic exploration did not require normative endorsement.
02
03 · Control and submission operate at different levelsParticipants could relinquish control within a role-play scene while maintaining the ability to revise, interrupt, or exit the digital interaction.
03
04 · Safety remains conditionalDisembodiment, editability, and nonhumanity reduced some material and relational risks, but platform dependence, algorithmic opacity, and model instability created others.
04
05 · AI as an “incomplete other”AI could feel responsive enough to matter relationally while remaining nonhuman enough to lessen some consequences of exposure. Boundaries of desire and the self became available for renewed interpretation.
05
CENTRAL ARGUMENT

Boundary → mediated encounter → boundary reconsideration

A conceptual summary of the thesis's finding that some limits expand, some become unstable, and others are reaffirmed. Not a deterministic causal model.

06 / ETHICS & SCOPE

Protecting the people behind the research.

Participants consented to the interviews and could decline or redirect disclosure. Interviews were analysed in Mandarin to preserve contextual meanings. Identifying details were removed or generalised, and public RedNote posts were not treated as automatically available for identifiable reproduction.

The sample consists of nine relatively young Chinese women; the findings describe the structure of these participants' lived experiences and are not estimates of how common AI kink practices are among Chinese women generally.

Public portfolio policy. No private transcripts, source-platform screenshots, participant profiles, or searchable intimate quotations are reproduced on this website. Full-text publication is a separate decision subject to privacy review.
